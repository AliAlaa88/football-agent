# 2026 FIFA World Cup

The **2026 FIFA World Cup** was the 23rd FIFA World Cup, the quadrennial international men's soccer championship contested by the national teams of the member associations of FIFA. The tournament took place from **June 11 to July 19, 2026**, with **Spain** winning the championship for the second time. 

It was jointly hosted by 16 cities across three North American nations: 11 in the United States, 3 in Mexico, and 2 in Canada. It was the first World Cup hosted by three countries and the first to expand to a **48-team format** (up from 32).

## Format & Expansion

Starting with the 2026 edition, the tournament expanded to 48 teams divided into **12 groups of 4 teams**. 
* **Qualification to Knockout:** Top 2 teams from each group + 8 best 3rd-place teams advanced to a new **Round of 32**.
* **Match Count:** Total matches increased from 64 to 104. Teams reaching the semifinals played 8 matches (up from 7).
* **Duration:** 39 days.
* **Rejected Formats:** An initial proposal for 16 groups of 3 teams (80 matches) was abandoned due to concerns over collusion and "circle of death" scenarios in 3-team groups.

---

## New Rules Introduced

1. **10-Second Substitutions:** Substituted players had 10 seconds to leave the field; otherwise, the incoming substitute waited 1 minute before entering.
2. **5-Second Restarts:** Visual 5-second referee countdown for throw-ins and goal kicks to stop time-wasting.
3. **Medical Treatment:** Outfield players receiving on-field medical care had to wait 1 minute off-field before returning.
4. **Expanded VAR:** VAR could review wrongly awarded corner kicks, clear mistakes on second-yellow red cards, and certain attacking fouls.
5. **Mouth-Covering Red Cards:** Players covering their mouth with a hand/shirt/arm while confronting an opponent were sent off to prevent concealed abuse.
6. **Leaving Field in Protest:** Automatic send-off for players/officials walking off in protest.
7. **Group Tiebreakers:** Head-to-head points between tied teams was prioritized over total goal difference across all group matches.
8. **Mandatory Breaks:** Mandatory 3-minute hydration breaks in each half of every match.
